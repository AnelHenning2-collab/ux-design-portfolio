Positioning

How to present the work

Lead with the publication pieces, not the course names. Hiring managers for publishing or ad layout roles will respond better to titles like Graduate Program Campaign, Analytics Report Spread, and Web Analytics Case Study than to raw assignment labels.

Each project page should show a hero mockup, one close-up crop of typography and grid detail, a short project summary, your role, and three specific design contributions such as hierarchy, adaptation, publication pacing, or production setup.
